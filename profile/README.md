## What we build

Logorythm is architecture intelligence for engineering teams, from static analysis: a service map from your code.

We turn your repositories into a service map. Insights make Conway's Law visible: where team boundaries and the real architecture diverge, where coupling becomes a bottleneck, and how the system evolves as the team ships.

Stop hunting callers in code and logs manually.

## How it works

1. **Connect GitHub** and pick the repos that make up the system
2. **Run a scan** with static analysis only (ephemeral processing)
3. **Explore the graph**: communities, hubs, blast radius, dependency cycles
4. **Read the insights**: structural signals of how the architecture is actually shaped, in plain language

## What the insights surface

Each scan turns graph metrics into decisions teams can act on:

- **Conway's Law, made visible.** Technical communities on the map vs how teams actually own services. Mismatch shows up before ownership fights do.
- **Clustering coefficient.** High local clustering is the signature of a distributed monolith: many deployables, one tangled system. Common when small teams keep carving microservices that still call each other in tight loops.
- **Coupling and modularity.** Rising cross-community edges and weak boundaries mean the architecture is getting harder to change independently.
- **Hubs and blast radius.** Orchestrators and single points of failure, with who depends on them.
- **Dependency cycles.** Contract changes that force coordinated releases across services.

That is how teams watch architecture evolve: not vanity dashboards, but whether coupling, clustering, and community boundaries are getting healthier or worse as the system grows.
